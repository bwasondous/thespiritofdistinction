Crafted With Krem

From late-night espresso martinis to holiday eggnog, Krem's smooth coconut, cream, and spice profile was made to be poured, stirred, and drizzled. A few ways to bring it to your table.

Cocktail Serves 1

Krem Espresso Martini

A modern classic, softened by Krem's coconut cream in place of the usual sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1½ oz Krem
  • 1½ freshly brewed espresso, cooled
  • 1 oz vodka
  • ¾ oz coffee liqueur
  • Ice
  • 3 coffee beans, for garnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker filled with ice.
  2. Shake hard for 15 seconds, until well chilled and frothy.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with three coffee beans.
Dessert Serves 1

Vanilla Ice Cream, Krem Drizzle

The simplest way to taste Krem on its own — poured warm over cold vanilla bean.

Ingredients

  • 2 scoops vanilla bean ice cream
  • 1½ oz Krem
  • Toasted coconut flakes
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Scoop the ice cream into a chilled bowl or coupe glass.
  2. Pour Krem (chilled) generously over the ice cream.
  3. Finish with toasted coconut and a dusting of cinnamon. Serve immediately.
Warm & Spiked Serves 1

Spiked Hot Chocolate

A fireside pour — Krem's spice and cream deepen a classic hot chocolate.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz hot chocolate, whole-milk based
  • 2 ½ oz Krem
  • Whipped cream
  • Grated nutmeg
  • 1 cinnamon stick

Method

  1. Prepare the hot chocolate as usual and let it cool slightly.
  2. Stir in the Krem off the heat.
  3. Top with whipped cream and a grating of nutmeg.
  4. Finish with a cinnamon stick for stirring.
Holiday Serves 4

Krem Eggnog (Krem-nog)

Holiday eggnog, reimagined with Krem's coconut cream and warm spice.

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups prepared eggnog
  • 6 oz Krem
  • 1 oz dark rum (optional)
  • Freshly grated nutmeg, to finish

Method

  1. Combine the eggnog and Krem in a pitcher and stir to combine.
  2. Add the dark rum, if using.
  3. Chill for 30 minutes to let the flavors meld.
  4. Serve over ice or in small glasses, topped with nutmeg.
Cocktail Serves 1

Krem Piña Colada

Krem stands in for half the cream, giving this island classic more depth.

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Krem
  • 1 oz white rum
  • 2 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz cream of coconut
  • 1 cup ice
  • Pineapple wedge and cherry, for garnish

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender with the ice.
  2. Blend until smooth and frosty.
  3. Pour into a chilled glass.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple wedge and cherry.
Dessert Serves 1

Krem Affogato

Hot espresso, cold vanilla, and a finishing pour of Krem — ready in under a minute.

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream
  • 1 shot espresso, hot
  • 1 oz Krem

Method

  1. Place the scoop of ice cream in a small glass.
  2. Pour the hot espresso directly over it.
  3. Finish with a pour of Krem.
  4. Serve immediately with a small spoon.

Krem is intended for customers 21 and older.
