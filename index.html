The Story

Born From The Warmth
of Haitian Tradition

Bwason Dous translates to "sweet drinks" in Haitian Creole, blending rich Caribbean tradition with contemporary innovation. From the beginning, our purpose has been simple: to create soulful, premium beverages crafted specifically for the most discerning palates.

Our flagship release is Krem. It stands as the purest, most balanced cream liqueur ever created. Inspired by traditional Haitian Kremas—a beverage that has marked moments of celebration and resilience since 1804—Krem offers a fresh, modern take on our culture’s timeless staple. Rather than reproducing the thick, heavy body of the classic recipe, we created a lighter, smoother expression. It honors the familiar, warm flavors of the original recipe while delivering them in a refined, remarkably balanced way.

Artisanal, distinct, and entirely its own, Krem is what liquid gold tastes like. A classic tradition thoughtfully reimagined, crafted with care and presented in its most polished form by the St. Vilûs & Louis family.
